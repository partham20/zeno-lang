\chapter{The Library at a Glance}\label{app-library}

Everything that comes with Zeno, on a few pages. The chapter that
explains each part is named beside it.

\section*{Functions that need no import}

\begin{center}
\begin{longtable}{lP{8.1cm}}
\toprule
Function & Gives \\
\midrule
\endhead
\verb|print(a, b, ...)| & shows the values on one line, with spaces between \\
\verb|input()|, \verb|input(question)| & the next line typed, or \verb|none| at the end (\hyperref[ch-reading-writing]{23}) \\
\verb|len(x)| & how many items, characters or keys \\
\verb|range(stop)|, \verb|range(start, stop, step)| & a list of whole numbers (\hyperref[ch-control-flow]{7}) \\
\verb|int(x)|, \verb|float(x)|, \verb|str(x)| & the value as another type; \verb|int| and \verb|float| of text may raise \verb|ValueError| \\
\verb|char(n)| & the character with Unicode number \verb|n| \\
\verb|abs(x)| & \verb|x| without its sign (\hyperref[ch-math]{24}) \\
\verb|min(...)|, \verb|max(...)| & the smallest or largest, of numbers or of a list \\
\verb|sum(xs)| & a list of numbers added up \\
\verb|u8(n)| and \verb|u16|, \verb|u32|, \verb|i8|, \verb|i16|, \verb|i32| & \verb|n| as a sized number, or \verb|OverflowError| (\hyperref[ch-sized-numbers]{18}) \\
\verb|u8.wrap(n)| and the others & the low bits of \verb|n|, never failing \\
\verb|allocate(T, n)|, \verb|free(p)| & memory by hand (\hyperref[ch-unsafe]{33}) \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
\end{center}
